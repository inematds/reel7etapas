Script template + video engine

An idea becomes a reel in 7 trigger steps

In short

reel7etapas is a template for writing short videos (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) and a tool that assembles those videos on its own. The script always follows the same 7-step order, designed to hold someone who is scrolling and to end with a simple ask: comment one word. It is for creators who want a repeatable path instead of starting from scratch with every video. To just use the template, reading the 7-step table is enough; to generate the video, the tool uses Nei's avatar in HeyGen, images made on your own computer and a Python script.

What it is

A fixed way to hold attention

Show the result before explaining the technology. People grasp the benefit in 2 seconds and stay to learn how to get it.

🧭 Ordered script

Seven steps, always in the same sequence. You change the topic, the structure stays.

🎞️ Assembled video

Talking avatar, one image per step, headline and panel that land on the exact word, and word-by-word captions, in 9:16.

🗺️ Map for teaching

The mapa command generates a sheet with the 7 steps side by side: the video frame, the trigger and the why.

The 7 steps

The order that makes people comment

Desire → fear of wasting money → ease → discovery → benefit → comment, opened by a visual hook. The examples come from the reel "Your own JARVIS" (Seu próprio JARVIS).

1 Hook→2 Desire→3 Fear→4 Ease→5 Discovery→6 Benefit→7 Comment

1 · Hook

The result anyone wants, in 2 seconds, in one huge sentence. "VOCÊ DORME. ELE TRABALHA." (YOU SLEEP. HE WORKS.) and the spoken line "Você sabia que o seu computador já pode trabalhar enquanto você dorme?" (Did you know your computer can already work while you sleep?)

2 · Desire

The person pictures themselves with what they want. "Já pensou em ter um funcionário dentro do seu computador?" (Ever thought about having an employee inside your computer?)

3 · Fear

The risk of losing money holds people more than the promise of gain. "Tem gente pagando uma fortuna por coisas que você já pode montar em casa." (Some people are paying a fortune for things you can already build at home.)

4 · Ease

Knock down the objection that makes people give up before trying. "E o melhor: não precisa ser programador!" (And the best part: you don't need to be a programmer!)

5 · Discovery

Name the solution and say what it does, in simple verbs. "Você pode criar o seu próprio JARVIS… conversa, lembra, pesquisa e executa." (You can create your own JARVIS… it talks, remembers, researches and executes.)

6 · Benefit

Close the picture of how life looks afterwards. "Um funcionário digital só seu, trabalhando do jeito que você ensinar." (A digital employee that's all yours, working the way you teach it.)

7 · Comment

One small, easy action: comment a word. "No inema.club tem vídeos e material… Comenta JARVIS que eu te mostro o caminho." (On inema.club there are videos and material… Comment JARVIS and I'll show you the way.)

🎨 Visual

Dark background, golden light, the computer front and center, yellow and white lettering. No futuristic robots, no sci-fi look.

🚫 Avoid

Jargon (AI, agent, model) before step 5, two theses in the same video, and a long CTA. One word to comment is enough.

Prerequisites

What needs to be up and running

To use only the template, nothing: it is the script. To generate the video with the tool:

reelcomenta + cardshorts

The engine reuses reelcomenta's transcription, face crop, HeyGen studio and Telegram delivery, and cardshorts' MusicGen.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/reelcomenta ~/projetos/reelcomenta

Local images

Image server running FLUX.2 klein, answering at localhost:8000/generate.

# different address?
export R7_IMAGEM_API=http://host:8000/generate

Python + ffmpeg

Pillow, OpenCV, PyYAML and ffmpeg. The avatar comes out of the HeyGen studio through the subscription, with a recorded approval.

pip install pillow opencv-python pyyaml
User guide · step by step

From text to delivered reel

Everything runs through reel7etapas.py. Each edition lives in edicoes/<date>-<topic>/.

1

Create the edition

Copies the JARVIS example script into a new folder. Rewrite the speech (550–700 characters, about 40 s) and the 7 steps.

python3 reel7etapas.py novo meu-assunto  # edicoes/2026-10-11-meu-assunto/roteiro.yaml
2

Write the steps

Each step has an ancora (anchor: the word in the speech that makes the step come in), headline, panel and the image cena (scene). gatilho (trigger) and por_que (why) only show up on the map.

  - id: medo
    ancora: gente
    linha1: "TEM GENTE PAGANDO"
    linha2: "UMA FORTUNA"
    painel: {titulo: "R$ $$$", texto: "por algo que\nvocê monta\nem casa"}
    cena: "stack of expensive bills on a dark desk, golden rim light"
3

Images and soundtrack

One image per step on the local FLUX.2 klein and an instrumental track on the local MusicGen.

python3 reel7etapas.py imagens edicoes/<pasta>
python3 ~/projetos/cardshorts/cardshorts.py musica edicoes/<pasta>/build/trilha.wav "warm hopeful cinematic underscore"
4

Preview before the avatar

Generates a sheet with one frame per step, at the estimated speech timing. Check for cropped text and wrong images.

python3 reel7etapas.py previa edicoes/<pasta>  # previa.jpg
5

Avatar in the HeyGen studio

Only runs with the recorded approval. Waits for the studio queue and downloads the MP4.

echo "aprovado" > edicoes/<pasta>/avatar/APROVADO_HEYGEN
python3 reel7etapas.py avatar edicoes/<pasta>
6

Assemble, check and map

Assembly finds the anchors in the avatar's actual speech and stops if it diverges from the script. The map becomes material for teaching the template.

python3 reel7etapas.py montar edicoes/<pasta>    # video.mp4
python3 reel7etapas.py conferir edicoes/<pasta>  # conferencia.jpg + volume
python3 reel7etapas.py mapa edicoes/<pasta>      # mapa.jpg + capa.jpg
7

Deliver

Sends the video through the Telegram bot. For YouTube, yt-pubx publishes it as a Short.

python3 reel7etapas.py enviar edicoes/<pasta> "legenda"
./yt-pubx publicar edicoes/<pasta>/video.mp4 --canal lives1 --contexto "..." --dry-run
