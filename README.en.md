## What it is

reel7etapas is a template for writing short videos (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) and a tool that assembles those videos on its own. The script always follows the same 7-step order, designed to hold someone who is scrolling and to end with a simple ask: comment one word. It is for creators who want a repeatable path instead of starting from scratch with every video. To just use the template, reading the 7-step table is enough; to generate the video, the tool uses Nei's avatar in HeyGen, images generated by Codex and a Python script.

**An idea becomes a 9:16 reel in 7 trigger steps** — hook → desire → fear → ease → discovery → benefit → comment — with Nei's avatar speaking, one image per step, a headline and panel that switch on the right word of the speech, word-by-word captions and the closing "COMENTA &lt;PALAVRA&gt;" (COMMENT &lt;WORD&gt;).

📖 Guide: **https://inematds.github.io/reel7etapas/guia/en/**

## The template

| # | Step | What it does | Example (JARVIS) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | shows the desired result in 2 s, before the technology | "VOCÊ DORME. ELE TRABALHA." (YOU SLEEP. HE WORKS.) |
| 2 | Desire | the person pictures themselves with what they want | an employee inside the PC |
| 3 | Fear | the risk of wasting money | some people are paying a fortune |
| 4 | Ease | knocks down the objection that makes people give up | you don't need to be a programmer |
| 5 | Discovery | names the solution, with simple verbs | create your own JARVIS |
| 6 | Benefit | how life looks afterwards | a digital employee that's all yours |
| 7 | Comment | one small action: comment a word | COMENTA JARVIS (COMMENT JARVIS) |

## Usage

```bash
python3 reel7etapas.py novo <slug>               # cria edicoes/<data>-<slug>/roteiro.yaml a partir do exemplo
echo "aprovado" > edicoes/<pasta>/avatar/APROVADO_HEYGEN
python3 reel7etapas.py fazer    edicoes/<pasta>  # imagens (Codex) → avatar → montar → validar → mapa, com portões
python3 reel7etapas.py imagens  edicoes/<pasta> 3 7   # refaz só as imagens 3 e 7
python3 reel7etapas.py validar  edicoes/<pasta>  # zona segura, legenda, ritmo, −14 LUFS, qa_short.py + folhas de revisão
python3 reel7etapas.py publicar edicoes/<pasta>  # bot do Telegram + YouTube (yt-pubx)
```

Gates that block: vertical, bright and non-repeating images; everything inside the Reels/Shorts safe zone; captions of at most 3 words and a font ≥ 64 px; an image change at least every 3.5 s; speech identical to the script; −14 LUFS; makeshorts' `qa_short.py` passed.

Standard closing line of the speech: "Tá tudo no inema ponto club. Ou comenta &lt;PALAVRA&gt; que eu te mando o link." (It's all on inema dot club. Or comment &lt;WORD&gt; and I'll send you the link.)

Full example: [`edicoes/2026-10-11-jarvis/roteiro.yaml`](edicoes/2026-10-11-jarvis/roteiro.yaml). For Claude Code or Codex, the skill is at [`skill/SKILL.md`](skill/SKILL.md).
